Services

From the first trace to the final report.

Every case follows the same discipline: secure the evidence, analyse it with validated methods, and document each step so the findings stand up to scrutiny.

Scene investigation

Systematic examination of the scene: locating, recording, collecting and preserving traces with a documented chain of custody.

Laboratory analysis

Biological, DNA and trace evidence examined in the laboratory under controlled, fully documented conditions.

Evidence examination

Detailed examination of physical evidence, with measurable, reproducible results and clear expert reporting.

Wildlife forensics

Science in service of nature.

We bring forensic rigour to cases involving protected species, poaching and the illegal wildlife trade, and support conservation work in the field with modern monitoring technology.

Discuss a wildlife case

  • Field sampling & evidence recovery

    Collecting and preserving samples so they stay intact and traceable from the moment they are found.

  • Species identification

    Identifying species from tissue, hair, feathers and wildlife products.

  • Acoustic & motion monitoring

    Sensor networks that detect activity in protected areas around the clock.

  • Remote sensing

    Satellite-linked and aerial data that extend investigations to remote terrain.

Dikaris Wildlife Forensic Kits

Field kits for rangers, researchers and enforcement teams, designed to collect and preserve wildlife evidence correctly from the very first step.

About Dikaris

Named for Dike, the goddess of justice.

Our name comes from Dike, the ancient Greek goddess of justice. Her stylised profile sits inside the geometric “D” of our mark: its linear structure stands for the precision, analysis and systematic methodology of forensic science, and the gold accent for the justice that work serves.
